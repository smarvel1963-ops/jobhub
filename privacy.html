Job Hub privacy policy

Job Hub is an app by Marvel Corp, part of the Hub family of apps. Last updated November 17, 2026.

The short version

What you put in Job Hub stays on your phone. There are no Job Hub accounts, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. The website version, opened in a web browser, adds one to an anonymous visit count when a page opens - only which page and where the visitor came from, with no cookies, no account and nothing you type. The installed app never does this. Your information is never sold, never used for advertising and never sent to employers or recruiters. Job Hub never applies for you and never signs in to any job site. The one thing that leaves your phone with your information in it is the optional AI helper, listed below.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type: your first name if you give it, the jobs you're looking for, the state you want to work in, remote, hybrid or on-site, the job type, the lowest pay you'd take, your current pay, your years of experience, the industries you like, how much each thing matters to you, your resume (name, headline, summary, each job you've held with its dates and what you did, and your skills), the jobs you save, their stage (Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer, Closed), interview and follow-up dates and follow-up notes, your notes, jobs you paste in yourself, jobs you hide, and the employers you follow. These are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them.

Your current pay is private. It is used on your phone only, to show how a job compares. It is never sent anywhere, the AI helper included.

Deleting: ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything deletes your Job Hub information on the phone. One saved job: open it → Take it off my pipeline.

Where the jobs come from

Once a day Job Hub collects open jobs from free, public sources: companies' own job boards (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby), remote jobs from Jobicy, and US federal jobs from USAJOBS. They are saved as one list on Job Hub's own site, and your phone downloads that list. Matching you to jobs happens on your phone: your resume and choices are never sent to get matches.

When you tap Apply, the job's own posting opens on the employer's site (or Jobicy or USAJOBS) under that site's own privacy policy. What you send them is between you and them.

A match score is a guide, not a promise. It shows its reasons - which words from the job are on your resume and which aren't - and marks anything not posted (like pay) as unknown instead of guessing.

Not career, legal or financial advice

Job Hub helps you find and track jobs. Pay ranges come from the postings themselves or from similar postings in today's list. Nothing in the app is career, legal or financial advice, and Job Hub is not an employer, a staffing agency or a recruiter.

Children

Job Hub is made for adults looking for work. It has no accounts and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
